# CelebrateHub — Business Model & Monetization (Events/Celebrations)

## Why quote-based works best
Event services/products have variables:
- Event date/time (availability)
- Venue constraints and logistics
- Guest count (pax)
- Theme/customization
- Add-ons (delivery/setup/tear-down)

Quote flow reduces friction and matches vendor workflows.

---

## Revenue options (recommended order)

### Option A — Featured placements (fastest)
- Free basic listings
- Paid featured slots:
  - Homepage featured vendors
  - Category top placement
  - “Verified” badge after checks
**Pros:** Simple, predictable for vendors, low operational burden  
**Cons:** Needs traffic to be valuable

Suggested pricing (SG):
- $39/mo Basic Featured (subcategory)
- $79/mo Category Featured
- $149/mo Homepage Featured (limited slots)

---

### Option B — Subscription tiers
- Free: 1 profile, limited listings, no analytics
- Pro: unlimited listings, inquiry management, featured boosts
- Premium: top placement + verified badge + priority support

Suggested tiers:
- Free
- Pro: $49/mo
- Premium: $99/mo

---

### Option C — Pay-per-lead (highly aligned)
Charge vendor per valid inquiry lead.
- $3–$8 per lead depending on category (wedding leads can be higher)
- Add “lead quality” rules (budget min, verified contact)
**Pros:** Vendors pay only when they get leads  
**Cons:** Need lead fraud prevention + dispute process

---

### Option D — Commission on paid bookings (later)
Add Stripe checkout for standardized packages (e.g., photobooth package).
- Platform fee: 5%–12%
- Hold deposit in escrow-like flow (optional)
**Pros:** Scales with volume  
**Cons:** More ops (refunds, disputes, cancellations)

---

## Go-to-market (SG-friendly)
1. Start with 3–5 categories (decor, cakes, catering, photobooth, rentals)
2. Onboard 30–60 vendors with clear profiles + portfolios
3. Promote via:
   - TikTok/IG Reels: “Top 5 birthday decor vendors”
   - SEO pages: “Balloon decoration Singapore”, “Halal catering”
   - Partnerships: venues, childcare centers, corporate HR

---

## Trust & quality controls
- Vendor approval required (pending → approved)
- Optional verification:
  - UEN + business registration
  - Portfolio checks
  - Customer reviews (phase 2)
- Clear SLA on response time (badge: “Replies < 2 hours”)

---

## KPI targets (first 90 days)
- Vendor response rate > 70%
- Quote-to-book conversion: 10%–25% (category dependent)
- Time-to-first-response: < 6 hours median
- Repeat usage: customers use platform for multiple events per year

---

## Product roadmap
Phase 1 (MVP): directory + listings + inquiry
Phase 2: vendor dashboard + inquiry inbox + analytics
Phase 3: reviews + verified purchases
Phase 4: payments for standardized packages + commissions
